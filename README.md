# Card Shops Near Me | cardshops.org

**[cardshops.org](https://www.cardshops.org)** is a nationwide directory of local trading card shops (TCG and sports card stores) across the US.

Find a card shop near you for:
- Pokemon TCG booster packs, singles and prerelease events
- Magic: The Gathering (MTG) Friday Night Magic, Commander nights and singles
- Yu-Gi-Oh! locals and tournaments
- One Piece Card Game, Disney Lorcana, Flesh and Blood, Digimon and Star Wars Unlimited
- Sports cards: baseball, basketball, football and hockey cards, wax boxes and grading drop-off

Search the full directory at **[www.cardshops.org](https://www.cardshops.org)**.
